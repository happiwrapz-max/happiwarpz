# Happiwrapz — Product Context & Durable Truth

## Product Purpose
Happiwrapz is an artisanal fresh flower and bespoke gift boutique studio based in Bangalore, India.

## Business Model & Operating Invariants
1. **Zero Online Payments**: The public storefront showcases floral arrangements, gift boxes, and hampers. Customers submit an **Order Request** specifying desired delivery date, timing slot, and personalized recipient note.
2. **Direct Store Owner Confirmation**: No upfront card, UPI, or wallet charge is ever accepted online. The studio owner connects with the customer via WhatsApp or phone call to verify fresh bloom availability, confirm custom requests, and finalize hand delivery.
3. **Database Architecture**: Powered by Supabase Postgres. Every order request generates an unguessable 6-character reference code (e.g., `HW-A3K7P9`).
4. **Delivery Coverage**: Hand delivery across Bangalore prime zones (Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout, Whitefield, MG Road, JP Nagar).

## Target Audience
Discerning gift givers celebrating milestones, anniversaries, birthdays, and corporate moments who seek quiet luxury, thoughtful handwritten cards, and morning-fresh floral arrangements.
